# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2025-12-17
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

Hobby Stock is a web application that helps hobby enthusiasts organize their supplies and plan projects by providing comprehensive inventory management and project tracking capabilities.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Hobby Enthusiasts**: Individuals who engage in various hobbies (crafting, electronics, woodworking, etc.) and accumulate supplies over time. They struggle with organizing their inventory and often don't know if they have all the supplies needed for a project.

- **Project Planners**: Hobbyists who plan multiple projects and need to track which items they have, which items they need, and how items are being used across different projects.

### User Personas

**The Organized Crafter** (25-55 years old)
- **Role:** Hobby enthusiast who engages in crafting, DIY projects, or creative hobbies
- **Context:** Has accumulated supplies over years but struggles to keep track of what they have and where items are stored
- **Pain Points:** Can't find supplies when needed, buys duplicate items, doesn't know if they have enough materials for a project
- **Goals:** Organize inventory efficiently, track project requirements, avoid unnecessary purchases

**The Electronics Hobbyist** (20-50 years old)
- **Role:** Electronics enthusiast or maker who works with components and parts
- **Context:** Manages many small components with different quantities and specifications
- **Pain Points:** Loses track of component quantities, doesn't know if they have the right parts for a circuit, components scattered across multiple locations
- **Goals:** Track component inventory accurately, link components to projects, maintain minimum stock levels

## The Problem

### Disorganized Supply Management

Hobby enthusiasts accumulate supplies over time but lack an effective system to track what they own, where items are stored, and current quantities. This leads to wasted time searching for items, duplicate purchases, and uncertainty about project readiness. Studies show that hobbyists spend 15-30% more on supplies due to poor inventory tracking.

**Our Solution:** Hobby Stock provides a centralized inventory management system with categorization, location tracking, and quantity monitoring to eliminate guesswork and reduce waste.

### Project Planning Uncertainty

When planning new projects, hobbyists often don't know if they have all the required supplies. They either delay starting projects to purchase items they may already own, or start projects only to discover missing critical components mid-project.

**Our Solution:** Hobby Stock links inventory items to projects, allowing users to check project requirements against available inventory and identify missing items before starting work.

### Lack of Supply Visibility

Without proper tracking, hobbyists can't see which items are running low, which items are used most frequently, or how supplies are distributed across active projects. This leads to unexpected stockouts and inefficient resource allocation.

**Our Solution:** Hobby Stock provides low-stock alerts, usage tracking through project-item relationships, and comprehensive dashboards showing inventory health and project status.

## Differentiators

### Integrated Inventory and Project Management

Unlike generic inventory apps or project management tools, Hobby Stock seamlessly connects inventory items to projects. Users can see exactly which items are needed for each project, track usage, and identify supply gaps. This integrated approach eliminates the need to switch between multiple tools and provides a complete view of hobby supply management.

### Hobby-Specific Features

Unlike enterprise inventory systems, Hobby Stock is designed specifically for hobby use cases with features like flexible quantity types (count, volume, percentage), location-based organization, and project-based item tracking. The system understands that hobby supplies aren't just commodities but creative resources tied to specific projects.

### Simple Yet Powerful

Unlike complex inventory systems with steep learning curves, Hobby Stock provides an intuitive interface that hobbyists can start using immediately while offering advanced features like low-stock alerts, search capabilities, and pagination for large inventories. This balance of simplicity and functionality makes it accessible to users of all technical levels.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Inventory Management:** Add, edit, and delete inventory items with detailed information including name, description, category, quantity, minimum quantity thresholds, unit price, location, and notes

- **Category Organization:** Organize items into categories for easy browsing and filtering, with support for custom categories and descriptions

- **Quantity Tracking:** Track item quantities with support for different amount types (count, volume, percentage) and decimal quantities for precise measurement

- **Location Management:** Store and track item locations to quickly find supplies when needed

- **Project Tracking:** Create and manage hobby projects with status tracking (active, completed, etc.), start/end dates, and detailed descriptions

- **Project-Item Linking:** Link inventory items to projects with quantity tracking to see which items are used in each project and how much is consumed

### Collaboration Features

- **Low Stock Alerts:** Automatic identification of items that have fallen below minimum quantity thresholds to prevent stockouts

- **Search and Filter:** Powerful search capabilities to find items by name, category, or location, with pagination support for large inventories

- **Dashboard Overview:** Comprehensive dashboard showing total items, active projects, categories, and low stock alerts at a glance

- **Project Supply Checking:** View all items required for a project and compare against available inventory to identify missing supplies before starting work
