# Hobby Stock Mission (Lite)

Hobby Stock is a web application that helps hobby enthusiasts organize their supplies and plan projects by providing comprehensive inventory management and project tracking capabilities.

Hobby Stock serves hobby enthusiasts who have accumulated supplies over time and struggle with organization, as well as project planners who need to verify they have all required supplies before starting work. Unlike generic inventory or project management tools, Hobby Stock seamlessly integrates inventory tracking with project planning, allowing users to link items to projects, track usage, and identify supply gaps in a single, hobby-focused platform.
